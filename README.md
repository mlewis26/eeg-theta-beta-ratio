## Results
- Mean theta (eyes open / closed): 44.10 / 46.00 µV²/Hz
- Mean beta (eyes open / closed): 6.20 / 8.61 µV²/Hz
- Mean theta/beta ratio (eyes open / closed): 7.23 / 5.88
- Theta (log): t = 0.46, p = 0.6556
- Beta (log): t = 3.84, p = 0.0040
- Ratio (raw): t = -1.43, p = 0.1858
- Ratio (log): t = -1.90, p = 0.0905
- Beta was higher with eyes closed in 10 of 10 subjects
- The ratio was higher with eyes open in 8 of 10 subjects

All tests are paired, closed minus open, so a negative t means the eyes-open value was higher.

## What I found
My beta prediction was wrong: beta was higher with eyes closed in every subject (p = 0.004).
Theta did not differ meaningfully between conditions. The theta/beta ratio was higher with
eyes open in 8 of 10 subjects, the opposite of my prediction, but the difference was not
statistically significant in this small sample, and the ratio change appears to be driven
by beta.
